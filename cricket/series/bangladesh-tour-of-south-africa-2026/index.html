Bangladesh tour of South Africa 2026

বাংলাদেশের দক্ষিণ আফ্রিকা সফর ২০২৬ · 2 Tests, 3 ODIs, 3 T20Is · Sun 15 Nov – Sun 13 Dec 2026

Bangladesh tour of South Africa 2026 is a series of 2 Tests, 3 ODIs, 3 T20Is between Bangladesh and South Africa, from Sun 15 Nov to Sun 13 Dec 2026. Next: South Africa vs Bangladesh, 1st Test starts Sun 15 Nov 2026 at 14:00 Bangladesh time at The Wanderers Stadium, Johannesburg.

Bangladesh play two Tests, three ODIs and three T20Is in South Africa from 15 November to 13 December 2026. The Tests count towards the 2025–27 World Test Championship.
