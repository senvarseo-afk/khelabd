Bangladesh's next 12 months

After the tour of South Africa (15 November to 13 December 2026), Bangladesh host England for two Tests in February 2027 and play a one-off Test at Lord's from 28 May 2027. They have already qualified directly for the ODI World Cup in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia, which starts on 2 October 2027; their first match is against England on 8 October. Bangladesh also host the 2027 Asia Cup (50 overs, dates not announced). The Bangladesh Premier League is not being held in 2026–27 and no return date has been announced.
