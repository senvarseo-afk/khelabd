What it means for Bangladesh

The two Tests in South Africa (15–19 and 23–27 November 2026) count towards the 2025–27 cycle. Each Test is worth 12 points for a win, 4 for a draw and 6 for a tie; teams are ranked by the percentage of available points they have won (PCT), so series of different lengths are comparable.

How the table works

PCT = points won ÷ points available × 100. The top two teams in PCT at the end of the cycle play the final. Slow over-rate penalties deduct points, which is why PCT can differ from a simple win count.
