No anniversary today. The next is in 52 days: First Test win in New Zealand, at Mount Maunganui (5 Jan 2022). 20 dates from Bangladesh cricket history are listed below by month, each with a card you can share on WhatsApp.

January

5 January · 2022 · Bangladesh tour of New Zealand 2021–22 · Mount Maunganui

First Test win in New Zealand, at Mount Maunganui

Ebadot Hossain's 6 for 46 set up an eight-wicket win over the World Test Champions, Bangladesh's first Test victory in New Zealand, Australia, England, South Africa or India.

10 January · 2005 · Zimbabwe tour of Bangladesh 2004–05 · Chattogram

First Test win, against Zimbabwe in Chattogram

Bangladesh won by 226 runs in their 35th Test; Enamul Haque jnr's 6 for 45 bowled Zimbabwe out for 154 in the second innings.

226 runsWhatsAppCard

February

9 February · 2020 · ICC U19 World Cup 2020 · Potchefstroom

Under-19 World Cup won in Potchefstroom

Akbar Ali's 43 not out guided a three-wicket DLS win over India in the final, Bangladesh's first World Cup title at any level.

1st world titleWhatsAppCard

March

9 March · 2015 · ICC World Cup 2015 · Adelaide

World Cup 2015: England beaten, first quarter-final reached

Mahmudullah's 103, Bangladesh's first World Cup hundred, and Rubel Hossain's two wickets in the 49th over won by 15 runs at Adelaide.

103WhatsAppCard
16 March · 2018 · Nidahas Trophy 2018 · Colombo

Nidahas Trophy: Mahmudullah finishes it in Colombo

Needing 12 off the last over against Sri Lanka, Mahmudullah hit 43 not out off 18 balls to win by two wickets with a ball to spare and take Bangladesh to the final.

43*WhatsAppCard
17 March · 2007 · ICC World Cup 2007 · Port of Spain

World Cup 2007: India beaten in Port of Spain

Mashrafe Mortaza's 4 for 38 and half-centuries from Tamim Iqbal, Mushfiqur Rahim and Shakib Al Hasan beat India by five wickets; India went out in the group stage.

4/38WhatsAppCard
19 March · 2017 · Bangladesh tour of Sri Lanka 2017 · Colombo

100th Test: first win over Sri Lanka, in Colombo

Bangladesh marked their 100th Test with a four-wicket win at the P Sara Oval; Shakib Al Hasan made 116 in the first innings and Tamim Iqbal 82 in the chase.

100th TestWhatsAppCard
23 March · 2022 · Bangladesh tour of South Africa 2022 · Centurion

First ODI series win in South Africa

Taskin Ahmed's 5 for 35 bowled South Africa out for 154 at Centurion and Bangladesh won by nine wickets to take the series 2–1.

5/35WhatsAppCard

April

7 April · 2007 · ICC World Cup 2007 · Providence, Guyana

South Africa beaten in the 2007 Super 8s

Mohammad Ashraful's 87 off 83 balls set up a 67-run win over the world's top-ranked ODI side at Providence, Guyana.

67 runsWhatsAppCard

May

17 May · 2019 · Ireland tri-series 2019 · Dublin

First multi-team trophy: tri-series final won in Dublin

Mosaddek Hossain's 52 not out off 24 balls chased a DLS target of 210 in 24 overs against West Indies at Malahide.

52* (24)WhatsAppCard
31 May · 1999 · ICC World Cup 1999 · Northampton

First World Cup win: Pakistan beaten at Northampton

Bangladesh beat Pakistan by 62 runs at the 1999 World Cup, the win that strengthened the case for Test status the following year.

62 runsWhatsAppCard

June

10 June · 2018 · Women's Asia Cup 2018 · Kuala Lumpur

Women's Asia Cup: Bangladesh's first continental title

Bangladesh Women beat India by three wickets off the last ball of the final in Kuala Lumpur, Salma Khatun's side ending India's run of six titles.

1st titleWhatsAppCard
18 June · 2005 · NatWest Series 2005 · Cardiff

Cardiff: Australia beaten by five wickets

Mohammad Ashraful's 100 off 101 balls and Aftab Ahmed's six in the final over beat the world champions in the NatWest Series.

100WhatsAppCard
21 June · 2015 · India tour of Bangladesh 2015 · Dhaka

First ODI series win over India

Mustafizur Rahman's 6 for 43 in the second ODI in Dhaka, after 5 for 50 on debut three days earlier, sealed the series with a match to spare.

6/43WhatsAppCard

July

20 July · 2009 · Bangladesh tour of West Indies 2009 · St George's

First away Test series win, 2–0 in the West Indies

Victory in St George's completed Bangladesh's first Test series win abroad, with Shakib Al Hasan captaining after Mashrafe Mortaza's injury in the first Test.

2–0WhatsAppCard

August

25 August · 2024 · Bangladesh tour of Pakistan 2024 · Rawalpindi

Rawalpindi: first Test win in Pakistan

Mushfiqur Rahim's 191 set up a ten-wicket win, Bangladesh's first Test victory over Pakistan at the 14th attempt and their first in Pakistan.

191WhatsAppCard
30 August · 2017 · Australia tour of Bangladesh 2017 · Dhaka

First Test win over Australia

Shakib Al Hasan took 10 wickets and made 84 as Bangladesh won by 20 runs in Dhaka.

20 runsWhatsAppCard

September

3 September · 2024 · Bangladesh tour of Pakistan 2024 · Rawalpindi

From 26 for 6 to a 2–0 series win in Pakistan

Litton Das (138) and Mehidy Hasan Miraz (78) rescued Bangladesh from 26 for 6, Hasan Mahmud took 5 for 43, and a six-wicket win completed a 2–0 sweep.

October

30 October · 2016 · England tour of Bangladesh 2016 · Dhaka

First Test win over England

Mehidy Hasan Miraz took 12 wickets in the match as England collapsed from 100 for 0 to 164 all out on the third evening; Bangladesh won by 108 runs in Dhaka.

12 wktsWhatsAppCard

November

10 November · 2000 · Inaugural Test · Dhaka

Bangladesh's first Test match begins in Dhaka

The inaugural Test against India started at the Bangabandhu National Stadium; Aminul Islam made 145, only the third century by a batter in his country's inaugural Test.
