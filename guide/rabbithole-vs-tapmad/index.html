Rabbithole vs Tapmad: Which Cricket Streaming App to Use in Bangladesh

By KhelaBD editorial · Published Fri 6 Nov 2026 · Updated Tue 10 Nov 2026

Rabbithole (র‍্যাবিটহোল) and Tapmad (ট্যাপম্যাড) are paid streaming apps available in Bangladesh that buy cricket rights series by series: Rabbithole was the digital platform for the 2026 Men's T20 World Cup in Bangladesh, and Tapmad has carried the Bangladesh Premier League and, in 2026, Bangladesh bilateral series such as the Tests in Australia. Neither is a permanent home for cricket, so decide per series from the confirmed broadcaster list rather than keeping both subscriptions all year.

Rabbithole

Rabbithole is the streaming arm of Rabbitholebd, whose YouTube network is one of the largest in Bangladesh. It has acted as the app home for ICC events in Bangladesh alongside T Sports on TV: for the 2026 Men’s T20 World Cup the ICC listed Total Sports Management as the Bangladesh licensee, with T Sports and Nagorik TV on TV and Rabbithole as the digital platform. It also posts highlights and full-match replays. ICC rights in Bangladesh for 2027 events, including the ODI World Cup, were put out to a new one-year tender in October 2026 and no partner had been announced as of 10 November 2026, so Rabbithole is not guaranteed to carry them. Subscriptions are sold monthly or per event. The app’s strength is reliability during ICC tournaments; its weakness is that outside those windows it may have no cricket at all.

Tapmad

Tapmad is a general entertainment streaming app that has bought sports rights, most notably the Bangladesh Premier League, which it carried together with T Sports, Nagorik TV and Akash GO. With the BPL on hold for 2026–27, Tapmad’s cricket in Bangladesh has come from other deals: in August 2026 the BCB named Tapmad as a streaming platform for Bangladesh’s two-Test series in Australia, which had no local TV coverage. Tapmad also sells other boards’ series and leagues in Bangladesh, such as the PSL and CPL. Live cricket on Tapmad is behind its paid subscription; there is no free tier for Bangladeshi viewers.

The practical rule: do not subscribe to either until the series page here shows it as the confirmed broadcaster. Rights are announced days before a series, and a subscription bought on a guess is money wasted.

Watching on TV instead

Both apps support casting to a smart TV. If you only want cricket on the big screen, check first whether T Sports or Nagorik TV has the TV rights for the same series; the TV feed is free-to-air and does not depend on your internet.

A warning about look-alike apps

Search results for “cricket live app” in Bangladesh are full of apps that promise free streams. They are either pirated feeds or vehicles for betting ads, both of which are illegal here, and several have been named by rights holders in takedown complaints. KhelaBD lists only the official apps and links only to their own stores.
