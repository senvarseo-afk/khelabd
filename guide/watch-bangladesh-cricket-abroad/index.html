How to Watch Bangladesh Cricket from Abroad (Legally)

By ShobMatch editorial · Published Fri 6 Nov 2026 · Updated Tue 10 Nov 2026

Bangladesh cricket is sold by country, so the T Sports, Toffee or Rabbithole stream you use at home is geo-blocked abroad and you need the official broadcaster for the country you are in. For Bangladesh's away series the host board's broadcaster usually sells worldwide digital rights to one streaming service per region; ShobMatch lists the confirmed option for the Middle East, Malaysia, the UK, the USA and Australia on each series page.

Why your usual app does not work overseas

Broadcast rights are licensed per territory. When T Sports buys a series, it buys Bangladesh only; the same match in Dubai or London belongs to a different company. Apps enforce this with geo-blocking, which is why a Bangladesh stream shows “not available in your region” the moment you land abroad. Using a VPN to get around it breaks the apps’ terms and often the stream still fails during big matches; this guide only covers the official routes.

Where to look, by region

The rights for a series belong to the home board. For Bangladesh’s tour of South Africa, Cricket South Africa sells the rights; for a home series in Dhaka, the BCB does. Each board announces an international partner per region, usually a week or two before the first match. Typical carriers in recent seasons:

RegionTypical carriers
Middle East (UAE, Saudi, Qatar)STARZPLAY, beIN Sports, Cricket channels on local IPTV packages
Malaysia and SingaporeAstro (Malaysia), Starhub or Mediacorp (Singapore)
United KingdomTNT Sports, Sky Sports (depends on the host board)
United States and CanadaWillow TV, ESPN+ for some boards
Australia and New ZealandKayo / Fox Sports, Sky Sport NZ

ICC events work differently: the ICC sells them itself, territory by territory. At the 2026 Men’s T20 World Cup, for example, the ICC’s official list had Sky Sports in the UK and Ireland, Willow TV in the USA and Canada, CricLife Max via STARZPLAY in the UAE and wider Middle East, Prime Video in Australia, and Astro Cricket in Malaysia. The 2027 World Cup list will be published by the ICC closer to the event.

The exact carrier for the current series is on its where-to-watch page here, with the date we last checked. If a region is blank, no official carrier has been confirmed yet.

Watching the Bangladesh commentary abroad

Bangla commentary is produced for the Bangladesh feed only. Overseas carriers use the host broadcaster’s English feed. Many fans abroad follow the match on ShobMatch’s live score page with the TV on mute, or listen to Bangla radio commentary where a station streams it; we list radio options when they exist.

Time zones

All times on ShobMatch are Dhaka time (UTC+6). A 14:00 Dhaka start is 10:00 in the UK (winter), 12:00 in the Gulf, 16:00 in Malaysia and 03:00 in New York. The schedule page can switch to your local time, and the calendar subscription puts each match into your phone’s calendar in your own zone.

What we never list

Pirated streams, mirror sites and “free live” APKs. They are illegal in Bangladesh and in most of the countries where Bangladeshi fans live, and they are the main way betting advertising reaches fans. If the official carrier in your region is expensive, the live score page here is free.
