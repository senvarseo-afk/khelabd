Bangladesh Cricket Calendar 2026–27: Every Series and Tournament Ahead

By KhelaBD editorial · Published Fri 6 Nov 2026 · Updated Tue 10 Nov 2026

Bangladesh's men's season starts with the tour of South Africa (two Tests from 15 November, three ODIs from 1 December, three T20Is from 10 December 2026), followed by two home Tests against England in February 2027, a one-off Test at Lord's from 28 May 2027, a five-ODI home series against Pakistan, and the ODI World Cup, for which Bangladesh have qualified directly; their first match is against England on 8 October 2027. The women tour New Zealand in December 2026 and England in September 2027. The BPL is on hold for 2026–27 with no return date announced.

November–December 2026: South Africa

Bangladesh’s longest tour of the season. Two Tests (Johannesburg from 15 November, Centurion from 23 November) count towards the World Test Championship. Three ODIs follow at East London, Gqeberha and Cape Town from 1 December, all starting 17:30 Dhaka time, and the three T20Is at Kimberley, Benoni and Centurion close the tour on 13 December. Bangladesh have already qualified directly for the 2027 World Cup, so these ODIs no longer affect qualification, though they still move the ranking used for seeding.

In parallel, Bangladesh Women play three T20Is and three ODIs in New Zealand from 10 to 23 December, with venues to be announced by New Zealand Cricket. Both tours have schedule pages on KhelaBD with start times converted to Dhaka time and a calendar subscription.

February 2027: England in Bangladesh

England tour Bangladesh in February 2027 for two Tests, in Mirpur and Chattogram, their first visit since 2016. This is Bangladesh’s last series of the 2025–27 World Test Championship cycle. The BCB has not yet published the exact dates.

May–June 2027: Test at Lord’s

Bangladesh play a one-off Test against England at Lord’s from 28 May to 1 June 2027, their first Test in England since 2010.

2027: Pakistan and the Asia Cup at home

Pakistan are due in Bangladesh for a five-ODI series in 2027; the series is listed in the BCB’s media-rights tender for 2026–27 home series, but dates have not been announced. Bangladesh are also due to host the 2027 Asia Cup in the 50-over format, under the Asian Cricket Council’s 2024 allocation; the ACC has not yet confirmed dates or venues.

October–November 2027: ODI World Cup

The 14-team World Cup in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia runs from 2 October to 21 November 2027. Bangladesh qualified directly on the ODI rankings at the 30 September 2026 cut-off and do not play the Qualifier. They are in Group B and open against England in Windhoek on 8 October; the World Cup guide lists all five group matches.

Bangladesh Women in 2027

After the New Zealand tour, the women’s side play three T20Is and three ODIs in England between 8 and 24 September 2027.

BPL

The Bangladesh Premier League is on hold for the 2026–27 season. In August 2026 BCB president Tamim Iqbal said the league would not be held this year while the board fixes its finances and franchise problems, and that the board would not rush its return; no return date has been announced. When the BCB confirms dates and teams, the BPL hub on this site goes live with squads, fixtures and live scores.

How this calendar is maintained

Fixtures come from the Sportmonks data feed and the boards’ announcements; a start time marked with an asterisk has not been confirmed yet. The Bangladesh schedule page is the live version of this guide and updates every 15 minutes.
