Cricket Glossary: English Terms with Bangla and Banglish Equivalents

By KhelaBD editorial · Published Fri 6 Nov 2026 · Updated Fri 6 Nov 2026

Scorecards use English terms, Bangladeshi commentary uses Bangla ones, and fan chats use Banglish spellings in between: a six is ছক্কা (chokka), a four is চার (char), out is আউট (out), and an over is ওভার (over). This glossary lists the terms you will meet on KhelaBD's match pages with their Bangla and Banglish forms, so a new fan can follow both the scorecard and the commentary.
