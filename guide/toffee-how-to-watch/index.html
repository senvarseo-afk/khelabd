Toffee App for Cricket: How to Watch, Data Packs and Costs

By KhelaBD editorial · Published Fri 6 Nov 2026 · Updated Tue 10 Nov 2026

Toffee (টফি) is the streaming app run by Banglalink that carries live sport in Bangladesh, including cricket series and tournaments it has licensed. The app is free to install and much of its live TV is free to watch; some content and higher quality sit behind a low-cost premium tier, and Banglalink users can buy Toffee-specific data packs so streaming does not eat the regular data allowance.

Who runs Toffee and what it carries

Toffee is operated by Banglalink, one of Bangladesh’s mobile operators, and has become one of the largest streaming apps in the country. It has carried the English Premier League, the 2026 FIFA World Cup alongside BTV, and cricket tournaments including the Bangladesh Premier League. Which cricket it shows depends on the rights it buys each season, so check the series page here for the current one.

Do you need a Banglalink SIM?

No. Toffee works on any network and on Wi-Fi. The Banglalink connection matters only for the special Toffee data packs, which let Banglalink customers stream without using their main data balance. On other networks you pay normal data rates; a two-hour T20 at medium quality uses roughly one gigabyte, so set the quality down if you are on a small pack.

Free vs premium

Toffee’s live TV channels are mostly free with ads. A premium tier, Toffee VIP, removes some restrictions and unlocks selected content, including some live sport. When the VIP packs launched in May 2025 they cost ৳76 for 7 days, ৳126 for 30 days, ৳276 for 90 days and ৳636 for a year; check the app for the current price before buying. Whether a specific cricket series is free or premium is announced per series; our where-to-watch entries say which.

Setting it up before match day

  1. Install Toffee from Google Play or the App Store and sign in with your phone number.
  2. Open the Live TV section and look for the sports channels or the event banner on the home screen.
  3. If you plan to watch on mobile data, buy the pack before the toss. Operators’ pack pages are slow when everyone buys at once.
  4. On a smart TV, check whether your TV’s app store has Toffee; otherwise cast from the phone.

Common problems

  • “Content not available in your region.” Rights are limited to Bangladesh. See the abroad guide.
  • Stuttering during a big match. Drop the quality one step and close other apps; the app is heavy on low-end phones.
  • Ads before the stream. Normal for the free tier; they cannot be skipped.

Toffee or T Sports?

If both carry the match, T Sports on TV is the most stable option and Toffee is the convenient phone option. For a match only one of them has, there is no choice to make, which is why our where-to-watch page exists.
