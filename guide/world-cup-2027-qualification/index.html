ODI World Cup 2027: Format, How Bangladesh Qualified and Their Fixtures

By KhelaBD editorial · Published Fri 6 Nov 2026 · Updated Tue 10 Nov 2026

The 2027 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup is a 14-team tournament hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia from 2 October to 21 November 2027. Hosts South Africa and Zimbabwe qualified automatically and the top eight other teams on the ICC ODI rankings on 30 September 2026 qualified directly; Bangladesh were one of them. The last four places are decided at the World Cup Qualifier in early 2027. Bangladesh are in Group B and open against England in Windhoek on 8 October 2027.

The tournament

The 2027 World Cup has 14 teams and 57 matches at 12 venues: eight in South Africa, three in Zimbabwe and one in Namibia (Windhoek). It runs from 2 October to 21 November 2027, with the final at Johannesburg. The ICC published the full schedule on 1 October 2026. The format is new and has four stages:

  1. Super Series (2–6 October, Windhoek). The three lowest-ranked qualifiers, which are the teams finishing second, third and fourth at the World Cup Qualifier, play each other once. The top team goes through.
  2. Group stage (7–24 October). Twelve teams in two groups of six, each playing the others in its group once. The top three in each group and the best fourth-placed team go through.
  3. Super 7. The seven remaining teams play each other once; the top four reach the semi-finals.
  4. Knockouts. Semi-finals in Cape Town on 17 November and Centurion (Tshwane) on 18 November, and the final in Johannesburg on 21 November.

How the 14 places were filled

  1. Hosts: South Africa and Zimbabwe qualified automatically as Full Member hosts. Namibia, the third host, is not a Full Member and did not get an automatic place; it has to come through the qualifying pathway.
  2. Rankings: the top eight teams on the ICC men’s ODI rankings on the cut-off date of 30 September 2026, other than the two hosts, qualified directly: India, New Zealand, Pakistan, Australia, England, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan and Bangladesh.
  3. Qualifier: the last four places go to the top four at the ICC Cricket World Cup Qualifier in early 2027. West Indies and Ireland, the next two on the rankings, go straight there and are joined by Associate teams. The Qualifier winner enters the group stage; the other three play the Super Series.

Bangladesh: qualified directly

Bangladesh finished the cut-off ninth on the overall ODI table, inside the direct places because hosts South Africa do not count towards the eight, and their direct place was confirmed once the cut-off passed. The ODIs in South Africa in December 2026 and later series no longer affect qualification, although they still move the ranking used for seeding.

Bangladesh are in Group B with New Zealand, South Africa, Sri Lanka, England and a Qualifier team. Their group matches, as published by the ICC:
