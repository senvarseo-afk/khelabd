How to Watch Cricket on T Sports: TV, App and YouTube

By KhelaBD editorial · Published Fri 6 Nov 2026 · Updated Tue 10 Nov 2026

T Sports is a free-to-air Bangladeshi sports TV channel owned by Bashundhara Group, and it also streams through the T Sports app and its YouTube channel. Most Bangladesh men's internationals in recent years have been shown on T Sports in Bangladesh; check the where-to-watch page for the exact series, because rights are sold series by series.

What T Sports is

T Sports (টি স্পোর্টস) launched in 2020 as Bangladesh’s first dedicated sports channel. It is a satellite channel carried by cable operators and DTH platforms across the country, and it runs a mobile app and a YouTube channel with several million subscribers. Nagorik TV, under the same media group, sometimes carries matches alongside it.

For cricket fans the channel matters because it has held the Bangladesh rights for most men’s international series and for ICC events in recent years, usually through a sub-licence from the global rights holder. Rights are bought one series or one tournament at a time, so “it was on T Sports last year” is not a guarantee for the next series. Our where-to-watch page for each series lists the confirmed broadcaster with the date we checked it.

Watching on TV

T Sports is a free-to-air channel: if your cable or dish package carries it, there is no extra charge for the cricket. Ask your cable operator for the channel number if you cannot find it, or search the channel list on your DTH box. During big matches the channel also runs a Bangla commentary feed, which is one reason many fans prefer it to international feeds.

Watching in the T Sports app

The app (Android and iOS) carries the live TV feed and match clips. Three things fans regularly complain about, and what to do:

  1. Buffering on mobile data. Switch the quality setting to the lowest option before the toss, not after the stream starts, and avoid watching through the Facebook in-app browser; open the app itself.
  2. Login problems during big matches. Log in before the match starts. If the app refuses to load, the YouTube stream (below) is the fallback.
  3. Paid live TV. The app has carried paid plans for live TV at times. Whether a given series is behind the paywall changes; this guide is updated when it does.

Watching on YouTube

The T Sports YouTube channel streams some matches live for viewers in Bangladesh and posts highlights within hours. Live streams on YouTube are geo-restricted to the territories covered by the rights, so the same stream may show “not available” outside Bangladesh. For overseas options see the guide on watching from abroad.

If T Sports does not have the series

Not every series lands with T Sports. In August 2026, for example, neither T Sports nor Nagorik TV showed Bangladesh’s two-Test series in Australia; it streamed on Tapmad instead. Toffee, Tapmad and Rabbithole have all carried cricket in Bangladesh in the last two years, and IPL has had no legal Bangladesh broadcaster since early 2026. Always check the series page here before match day; it only lists official broadcasters and never streaming links.
