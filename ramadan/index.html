How Ramadan changes match times

For home internationals in Ramadan the BCB usually brings start times forward so that a T20I finishes before iftar, or schedules an ODI so that the innings break falls at iftar; day matches may start as early as 09:30 Dhaka time. Players who are fasting are allowed drinks breaks at iftar during play, and broadcasters pause for the adhan. Friday fixtures often start later to clear Jummah prayers, which are around 13:15 in Dhaka. For away tours the Bangladesh players follow the host's schedule, and the times on this page are for fans watching from Bangladesh.

Watching during Ramadan

Evening matches in the Gulf, South Africa and Europe often start around iftar in Dhaka; the fixture rows on this site show the iftar time next to each match so you can plan. Sehri times matter for late-night matches in the Americas and the Caribbean, which can run past 04:00 Dhaka time. Eid al-Fitr is a public holiday, and the BCB does not schedule home matches on Eid day.
